About Catch Fishing Services

A dedicated fishing and remedial services specialist

Catch Fishing Services B.V. is based in Emmen, the Netherlands, and supports drilling and workover operations across Europe and beyond. We focus on a single discipline — fishing, milling, and downhole remedial work — and we do it with the depth of experience that focus brings.

Our engineers and tool pushers are deployed wherever the job requires, backed by an in-house workshop that builds and maintains the tools we run. From planning to execution, every job is handled by people who have seen the problem before and know how to solve it.

Products & Services

Fishing, milling & remedial solutions

From standard fishing runs to custom-engineered tooling, our service range covers every stage of a downhole recovery or remediation operation.

Fishing

Recovery of stuck pipe, tools, and junk from the wellbore using overshots, spears, and grapples matched to the job.

Milling

Removal of metal obstructions, packers, and debris by milling them down to junk that can be circulated or fished out.

Section Milling

Controlled removal of a full casing section to expose formation or open a window for sidetrack and abandonment work.

Washover

Pipe is run over a fish to release it from cuttings or debris, freeing the stuck assembly without milling it apart.

Underreaming

Enlargement of the wellbore below restrictions using expandable arms, opening hole diameter for casing or completion.

Wellbore Cleanout

Removal of fill, scale, and debris to restore full wellbore access ahead of completion or workover operations.

Custom Tooling

Non-standard fishing or milling problems engineered and manufactured in-house for a job-specific solution.
